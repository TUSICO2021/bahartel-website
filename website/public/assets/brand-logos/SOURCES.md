# Logo sources

Lenovo, NetApp, Huawei, Juniper Networks, and NVIDIA vector marks were obtained from the Simple Icons project (CC0-1.0), using its published SVGs and metadata colors. Sources: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons and https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@latest/icons/ . Geometry is unchanged. Empty SVG margins may be trimmed by adjusting viewBox for display.

Distributor names shown in the backing network come from the supplied company table. They are typographic company names, not recreated official logo artwork.
